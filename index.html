About CloudAM

Managing the cloud–edge continuum

Cloud computing, virtualization, and virtualized applications have generated substantial interest for more than a decade, enabling the development of new architectures, techniques, and mechanisms for cloud and edge computing.

With the deployment of edge infrastructures—including fog computing, mobile edge computing, and resource-constrained intelligent devices—cloud and edge platforms increasingly work together as a computing continuum. These systems must support diverse users and applications while meeting performance, dependability, energy, privacy, and quality-of-service requirements.

CloudAM 2026 provides a forum for researchers and practitioners to discuss new systems, methods, and approaches for managing cloud, edge, fog, and continuum infrastructures and applications. We welcome original work that advances autonomic, intelligent, sustainable, secure, and distributed management across the continuum.

Call for Papers

Topics of interest

Topics include, but are not limited to, the following areas.

01

Autonomic & intelligent management

Self-adaptation, AI-driven operations, autonomous orchestration, intent-based management, and zero-touch systems.

02

Cloud–edge continuum

Architectures, APIs, scheduling, placement, resource allocation, load balancing, and service migration.

03

Applications & services

Microservices, serverless computing, scientific workflows, eScience, IoT, smart cities, and digital twins.

04

Data & observability

Distributed data management, complex event processing, monitoring, telemetry, and performance analytics.

05

Performance & dependability

Performance modelling, scalability, QoS/QoE, resilience, fault tolerance, and lifecycle management.

06

Security & sustainability

Security, privacy, trust, green computing, energy optimisation, and environmental impacts of the continuum.

07

Networks & virtual resources

Software-defined networking, virtual slices, network-aware management, and virtualized hardware resources.

08

Multi-cloud ecosystems

Hybrid clouds, multi-cloud and inter-cloud systems, federations, business models, and policy-driven management.

Submission

Share your work with CloudAM 2026

CloudAM invites original and unpublished research contributions relevant to cloud, edge, fog, and computing-continuum management.

Submission link: papers are submitted through the CloudAM 2026 EasyChair track.

Paper requirements

  • PDF format, written in English.
  • Maximum of 6 pages, including references.
  • Single-spaced, double-column IEEE conference format.
  • Double-blind review: remove author-identifying information.
  • Original work not under review or published elsewhere.
  • At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present in person.
